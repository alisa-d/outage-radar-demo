# Uncertain matches are "Possibly affected"; unplaceable city outages are shown but not alerted

Provider Notices describe areas in loose prose ("в района на ЖП гара", "част от кв. Аспарухово", "клиентите, електрозахранени от ТП 314"), so a yes/no match would force a choice between missed outages and false alarms. We use three Match Results instead: Affected, Possibly affected and Not affected (plus No data when a Provider's data is unavailable). Subscribers are alerted on Possibly affected as well, with wording that says so and the original Notice attached, because missing an outage costs a resident more than an honest "this may affect you". The exception is an Outage that cannot be placed at all within a city (for example, only a transformer station is named). It becomes Unlocated: it is shown in the Region summary and in address checks, but no Alert is sent, because flagging an entire city would train people to ignore our Alerts. Every Unlocated Outage goes to operator review, and placing it there releases the Alerts. In a small village, by contrast, an unplaceable Outage is Possibly affected for the whole Settlement.
